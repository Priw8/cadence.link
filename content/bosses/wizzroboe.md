![](/img/bosses/wizzroboe.png)

The boss of [Frozen Grotto](/dungeons/frozen-grotto/).
Has 10 hearts, 12 on increased difficulty.

The fight has 2 phases - once half of the health is depleted (visually indicated by the boss changing color a bit), it transitions into phase 2 which always begins with the rush attack.

## General strategy

Wizzroboe's phase 1 is one of the most random bosses in the game and it's generally advised to spend as little time as possible in this state. The quick kills above very good but unfortunately not applicable in most speedruns due to needing a bottle with a bomb fairy. Because of that, it's important to know some less fast but still good enough ways to get through the fight.

### Early first hit

If all you have is a dagger-range weapon, it's impossible to hit Wizzroboe before it warps away at the start. This hit can often save a cycle, so it's good to come into the fight prepared. Ways to hit the boss early include:

- Spear/Flail/Longsword/Rapier without Glass Slippers/Heavy Boots (as they prevent sliding into the arena 1 tile higher): just walk up and punch the boss
- Pegasus Anklet: can either be used to sidestep the problem of not sliding into the fight due to boots (so that weapons above can hit), or for 2 damage on its own. **There is some jank related to using the Anklet on beat 1 of boss fights**, see [here](/general/issues/#first-beat-of-boss-fights) for details.
- Bow: can always be shot to deal 1 damage at the start. However, 1 damage does not always actually make the transition into phase 2 faster.
- Greatbow: same as above but better as it deals 2 damage. However it's a lot harder to end up with a Greatbow in a speedrun.
- Bombchu: you can kick a Bombchu to have it hit Wizzroboe before it teleports. Requires sliding into the fight. **You have to move one tile up before placing and kicking it**. It would seem that this shouldn't make a difference but it does because of beat 1 jank.

### Miscellaneous ways to make the fight faster after 1st teleport

During phase 1, Wizzroboe chooses one of few possible attacks. It's only possible to hit it once during each of them (apart from a rare situation where it may be possible to do a weapon attack after a bow hit while sliding on ice, but this usually doesn't actually work and exact conditions are unknown). Because of that it's important to maximize the damage you deal on each cycle. The following can help:

- **Scroll of Electricity** can be used as a pseudo damage up for this fight. Because Wizzroboe occupies 4 tiles, hitting it with an electric attack causes electricity to spread onto other tiles for additional 1 damage.
- Similarly, **Scroll of Need** can be used for its "Damage enemies" effect. This deals a whopping 5 damage, allowing for an instant transition to phase 2 in the easy version of the fight. Note that you need to be cardinally adjacent to the boss for this.
- **Shooting the bow** can be done while sliding on ice. Because this only deals 1 damage most of the time, it's primarily useful when you only need a bit of damage, as hitting with a higher-damage weapon is always preferred.
- **Bombchus** and **Anklet** can be used for pretty easy 3 and 2 damage hits. Regular **bombs** work too, but you have to be quite fast to have them explode before the boss teleports.
- **Dealing with Wizzrobes**: one of the random attacks the boss can use summons a bunch of Wizzrobes. This is extremely slow and therefore not very good. However, when the boss begins the next attack actually depends on the amount of enemies/projectiles around your character, so it can be (sometimes quite significantly!) sped up by trying to hang around in a spot without any Wizzrobes and projectiles. This is even more important if you transition into phase 2 during this attack, as Wizzroboe may completely cancel its normal post-phase 1 rush attack if it's unable to start attacking for too long.
- **Never bump the boss by sliding on ice**. It will teleport before you can actually attack.

For phase 2:

- Try to deal as much damage as possible during the rush attack the boss does after the phase transition. This is most easily doable with **Zelda**, as she can use Nayru's love to block the hits and immidiately counter-attack with any weapon without moving. If Nayru's love can't be used (either due to lack of Stamina or lack of Zelda), some weapons can hit the boss depending on which of the 2 attacks it did:
  - For the attack that requires **stepping away**: Spear, Rapier, Longsword and Flail can be used to hit from 2 tiles away.
  - For the attack that requires **stepping to the side**: Broadsword, Greatshovel and Flail can hit while standing diagonally away from the boss.

  This makes the Flail the only weapon that can hit in both cases.
- After the last hit of the rush attack, there is enough time to place a bomb and have it explode to deal 3 damage to the boss.
- Ideally, the boss should be killed during or immidiately after the rush attack.

### Deciding whether to actually fight Wizzroboe now 

Because Frozen Grotto has the easiest floor 1 skip, it's often the case that it's the first dungeon done in a run. It's typically best to complete the dungeon including the boss immidiately, to avoid wasting time warping around Hyrule later. However, in some situations the boss can be so unbearably slow that it's best to leave it for later. The question you should ask yourself is how many cycles you expect the fight to take (following assumes easy version of boss): 

- If it's 1 or 0 (5+ damage early hit, most often glass rapier with damage up), there is no reason to delay the fight.
- If it's 2 (either 3+ damage or 2 damage + guaranteed early hit) it's usually better to just do the fight.
- If it's 3 (2 damage, or 2 damage early hit and 1 damage) it may be worth doing the fight if your routing relies on moving east/west from Lake Hylia in the fixed beat mode you get after the boss is killed. Otherwise, probably not.
- If it's 4 or more, leaving the boss for later until you're more prepared will almost certainly save time.

You should also consider the extra cycles you will take if you can't kill phase 2 during the rush attack.

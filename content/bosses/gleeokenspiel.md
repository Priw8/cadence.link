![](/img/bosses/gleeokenspiel.png)

The boss of [Temple of Storms](/dungeons/temple-of-storms/).
Has 6 hearts, 8 on increased difficulty.
The heads have 6 hearts each, 8 on increased difficulty.

## General strategy

Explosives (bombchus/bombs) greatly help at the start of the fight:

{{< video gleeok-bombchu.mp4 >}}
{{< video gleeok-bomb.mp4 >}}

If you have no Glove to throw the bomb for any reason (for example in Dungeon Mode), you can kick the bombs but you have to be careful not to get caught in the explosion:

{{< video gleeok-bomb-kick.mp4 >}}

Outside of dungeon mode, there is a pot that's guaranteed to have 3 bombs inside, so they are always available for the fight. [Item duplication](/tech/item-duplication) can be used to obtain 6 bombs instead of 3.

![](/img/bosses/gleeok-guaranteed-bomb-pot.png)

**Important note**: the heads behave differently in fixed beat mode. Normally, a head won't move to middle and block explosives at the beginning of the fight. However, this can happen in fixed beat mode for some reason.

Generally, using explosives allows to consistently detach at least 2 heads at the beginning of the fight. It's possible to hit more, but it's random whether the heads that start on the outside move into the explosion radius. This works for both the easy and hard version of the boss, although hard version requires more bombs (which is not a problem due to the aforementioned guaranteed bomb pot).

Outside of this, being able to detach heads in 2 hits makes the fight a lot easier (so you need at least 3 damage for the easy version, and at least 4 for the hard version).
